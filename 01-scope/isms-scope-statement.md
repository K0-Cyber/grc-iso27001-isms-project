# ISMS Scope Statement

## Purpose

This document defines the boundaries and applicability of the Information Security Management System (ISMS) for EugTech Solutions Ltd.

This supports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Clause 4.3.

## Organisation

| Field | Details |
|---|---|
| Organisation | EugTech Solutions Ltd |
| Sector | Technology — Cloud Consultancy and Managed Services |
| Size | 50 employees across London and remote UK |
| Clients | 20 SME clients in financial services, healthcare, and retail |
| Standard | ISO/IEC 27001:2022 |
| ISMS Scope Owner | Information Security Manager |

## ISMS Scope Statement

The ISMS of EugTech Solutions Ltd encompasses all information assets, processes, systems, and people involved in the design, delivery, and support of cloud consultancy and managed IT services to external clients.

The scope includes:

- All cloud infrastructure operated on behalf of clients, including Azure and AWS tenants
- Internal corporate IT systems, including Microsoft 365, Azure AD, laptops, and mobile devices
- Source code repositories and development environments
- All 50 employees and contractors operating within UK jurisdiction
- Physical premises, including the London office and home-working environments

## Key Assets Within Scope

- Client data
- Source code
- Azure and AWS cloud infrastructure
- Microsoft 365
- Staff laptops and mobile devices
- Source repositories
- Development environments
- Internal business systems

## Exclusions

| Exclusion | Justification |
|---|---|
| Personal devices not used for company business | These devices are not used to process company information |
| Client-owned infrastructure where EugTech has no administrative control | EugTech does not have the authority to implement or manage controls on these systems |

## Scope Boundary

The ISMS applies to all cloud services, managed services, and internal IT systems supporting client delivery.

The scope includes people, technology, processes, information assets, physical environments, and third-party dependencies where EugTech has operational responsibility.

## Review

The ISMS scope will be reviewed:

- Annually
- After significant organisational change
- After major changes to cloud services
- After acquisition or disposal of business units
- After significant regulatory or contractual changes
