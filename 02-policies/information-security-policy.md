## 1. Purpose

EugTech Solutions Ltd is committed to protecting the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of information belonging to the organisation and its clients.

This policy establishes the framework for managing information security risks across all operations.

## 2. Commitment

EugTech commits to:

- Implementing and maintaining an Information Security Management System (ISMS) aligned to ISO/IEC 27001:2022
- Identifying and managing information security risks through a structured risk assessment process
- Complying with all applicable legal, regulatory, and contractual information security requirements, including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018
- Providing information security training and awareness to all staff
- Continually improving the effectiveness of the ISMS through regular review and internal audit

## 3. Scope

This policy applies to all EugTech employees, contractors, and third parties who access company or client information systems.

The full ISMS scope is defined in the ISMS Scope Statement.

## 4. Policy Statements

### 4.1 Information Assets

All information assets must be:

- Classified
- Inventoried
- Assigned an owner

### 4.2 Access Control

Access to information must be granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed quarterly.

### 4.3 Security Incidents

All security incidents must be reported immediately to the Information Security Manager.

### 4.4 Remote Working

Remote and home working is permitted subject to the Remote Working Policy.

### 4.5 Supplier and Third-Party Access

Supplier and third-party access to company data must be governed by a signed agreement.
