# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines the acceptable use of EugTech Solutions Ltd information and information assets.

It supports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control A5.10 — Acceptable Use of Information and Other Associated Assets.

## Scope

This policy applies to:

- Employees
- Contractors
- Third parties authorised to use EugTech information or systems
- Company-managed information assets

## Acceptable Use Requirements

All users must:

- Use EugTech information and systems only for authorised business purposes
- Follow the Information Security Policy
- Protect company and client information according to its classification
- Use only the access permissions assigned to them
- Immediately report suspected security incidents to the Information Security Manager
- Follow applicable remote-working requirements when working away from company premises

## Information Classification

EugTech uses four information classification levels:

| Classification | Description |
|---|---|
| Public | Information that may be freely shared externally |
| Internal | Information intended for staff use and not for external sharing without approval |
| Confidential | Business-sensitive information shared only on a need-to-know basis |
| Restricted | Highest-sensitivity information requiring tightly controlled access |

Examples of Restricted information include:

- Client personal data
- Passwords
- Cryptographic keys
- Sensitive legal information

## Access to Information

Access must:

- Be based on business need
- Follow least-privilege principles
- Be reviewed quarterly
- Be removed when no longer required

## Information Assets

Information assets must be:

- Recorded in the asset inventory
- Assigned an owner
- Classified appropriately
- Protected according to their classification

## Security Incidents

Users must immediately report suspected information security incidents to the Information Security Manager.

## Acknowledgement

This policy is:

- Provided during onboarding
- Reissued annually
- Subject to signed staff acknowledgement
