# Remote Working Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines information security requirements for remote and home working at EugTech Solutions Ltd.

It supports ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A control A6.7 — Remote Working.

## Scope

This policy applies to all employees and contractors who access EugTech information or systems away from EugTech premises.

## Remote Working Requirements

Remote workers must:

- Use the approved VPN when remotely accessing EugTech systems
- Use encrypted company-managed devices
- Protect confidential information when working in public locations
- Maintain a clean screen when working in public
- Follow all Information Security Policy requirements
- Immediately report lost, stolen, or compromised devices

## Company Devices

Company laptops must use:

- Full-disk encryption using BitLocker
- Mobile Device Management (MDM)
- Remote wipe capability
- Automatic screen locking
- Approved security controls

Screens must automatically lock after five minutes of inactivity.

## Removable Media

Where USB storage is authorised, encrypted USB devices must be used.

## Personal Devices

Personal devices not used for company business are outside the ISMS scope.

Devices used to access company information must comply with applicable EugTech security and management requirements.

## Protection of Information in Public

Users must take appropriate precautions when working in:

- Public transport
- Cafes
- Shared workspaces
- Other public environments

Confidential or Restricted information must not be exposed to unauthorised persons.

## Incident Reporting

Lost devices, suspected compromise, unauthorised access, or other remote-working security incidents must be reported immediately to the Information Security Manager.

## Evidence

Implementation evidence may include:

- VPN logs
- MDM compliance reports
- BitLocker compliance reports
- Remote Working Policy acknowledgement
